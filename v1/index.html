Abstract

Doctoral research at LOMA (UMR-5798, CNRS, Bordeaux) on the electronic structure of infinite-layer and layered nickelates Rn+1NinO2n+2 — constructing effective multi-orbital models that separate genuinely nickelate physics from the cuprate paradigm, and identifying antiferromagnetic and charge-order instabilities driven by Fermi-surface nesting.

The through-line of my work is the same everywhere: reduce a many-body problem to the minimal model that still contains its physics, then compute it honestly — Green's functions, DMFT, DFT (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO), exact diagonalization, molecular dynamics — on HPC infrastructure, with pipelines automated end to end. The same discipline transfers directly to applied problems: ML-driven drug-design screening, radiation-shielding nanomaterials, and Ising-mapped portfolio optimization.

Every visual on this page is a working numerical simulation, not an illustration.

Nickelate Fermiology

Zero-frequency spectral weight A(k, 0) of a two-band model for layered nickelates — the second Fermi pocket whose emergence and tunability under doping/pressure is the subject of my J. Phys.: Condens. Matter (2025) paper. Raise μ to pull the interlayer band through EF — an electron pocket emerges at Γ — then dial V to hybridize the bands and gap the crossings.

MODULE 02 ● IDLE

Spin-Glass Annealer

Portfolio optimization mapped onto an Ising spin glass — assets become spins si = ±1, covariances become couplings Jij, and the optimizer is simulated annealing inspired by quantum statistical mechanics. Frustrated bonds (disorder Δ) create the rugged energy landscape; the cooling schedule decides whether you find the ground state or a glassy trap.
